These are Cam Post Solutions Limited's standard business terms. They should be read together with the applicable quotation or order confirmation and the agreed project specification. Where a higher-ranking project document conflicts with these Terms, clause 2 sets out the order of precedence.

1. Definitions

In these Terms, the following definitions apply:

“Agreed Specification” means the functionality, machine requirements, deliverables and NC output behaviour stated in the Supplier’s quotation, order confirmation or other written project document accepted by both parties.

“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.

“Customer” means the business purchasing the Services and Deliverables.

“Deliverables” means the Post Processor and any documentation or other files expressly listed in the Agreed Specification.

“Delivery Date” means the date on which the Supplier first makes the Deliverables available to the Customer for acceptance testing.

“Machine Configuration” means the identified machine tool, CNC controller, options, accessories, parameters, kinematics and operating requirements.

“Post Processor” means the CNC post processor software and customer-specific configuration developed under the Agreement. Source files are included only where expressly stated in the Agreed Specification.

“Services” means development, delivery and the support expressly described in the Agreement.

“Supplier” means CAM Post Solutions Limited.

2. Agreement and Order of Precedence

2.1 The agreement consists of, in descending order of precedence: (a) any signed change order; (b) the quotation or order confirmation, including the Agreed Specification; and (c) these Terms. A higher-ranking document prevails only to the extent of a conflict.

2.2 A Customer purchase order is accepted for administrative purposes only. Any terms printed on or linked from it do not apply unless expressly accepted in writing by a director of the Supplier.

2.3 The Agreement begins when the Customer signs the acceptance section, confirms acceptance in writing, issues a purchase order referring to the quotation, or instructs the Supplier to begin work—whichever occurs first.

3.1 The Supplier will develop and supply the Post Processor for the CAM system and Machine Configuration in the Agreed Specification. It is not intended for any other machine, controller, configuration, CAM product or release unless agreed in writing.

3.2 The Customer shall provide accurate and complete machine manuals, sample NC programs, controller information, machine parameters, kinematic data, tooling requirements and access to suitably trained personnel as reasonably required.

3.3 The Supplier may rely on information supplied by the Customer, machine manufacturer, CAM vendor or other third party. The Supplier is not responsible for delay, rework or defects caused by inaccurate, incomplete or late information.

3.4 Any delivery date is an estimate unless the quotation expressly states that time is of the essence. A Customer or third-party delay extends the timetable by a reasonable corresponding period.

4. Price, VAT, Expenses and Payment

4.1 The fixed price covers only the Services and Deliverables in the Agreed Specification, including the 90-day support period in clause 8. All prices exclude VAT, which will be charged at the applicable rate.

4.2 Reasonable travel, accommodation and other out-of-pocket costs are excluded unless expressly included in the quotation. Any chargeable costs will be agreed in advance.

4.3 Payment is due on delivery and must be made within the period stated on the invoice. The Customer may not withhold or set off payment except where required by law.

4.4 If an undisputed amount is overdue, the Supplier may suspend further work and support after giving written notice. The Supplier may claim statutory interest, compensation and reasonable recovery costs available under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

4.5 Ownership of customer-specific elements of the final Post Processor does not transfer until cleared payment is received in full.

5. Changes and Additional Work

5.1 A change to the Agreed Specification must be agreed in writing and may affect price and delivery. The Supplier need not begin additional work until scope, charges and timing are agreed.

5.2 Additional work includes new machine functions or cycles; another machine or controller; requirements introduced after work begins; changes caused by hardware, parameters, controller or CAM updates; additional automation, macros or features; on-site attendance not included in the quotation; and work requested after the support period.

5.3 Unless otherwise agreed, additional work is charged at the Supplier’s then-current hourly or daily rate.

6. Delivery, Testing and Acceptance

6.1 The Customer must test the Deliverables promptly in a safe, controlled environment and notify the Supplier in writing of any reproducible material failure to meet the Agreed Specification, with the NC file, CAM file where reasonably available, post configuration, machine details and sufficient steps to reproduce it.

6.2 The Deliverables are accepted on the earliest of: (a) written acceptance; (b) production use other than controlled proving; or (c) 15 Business Days after the Delivery Date if the Customer has not reported a reproducible material non-conformity.

6.3 A minor defect that does not materially prevent the agreed use does not delay acceptance. Acceptance does not remove the limited warranty in clause 9.

6.4 If the Customer delays testing, the 90-day period still runs from the Delivery Date unless the Supplier agrees an extension in writing.

7. Safe Proving and Operational Responsibility

7.1 The Post Processor generates NC output from CAM data and configuration information. The Supplier does not control the CAM programming, machine condition, machine parameters, setup, tooling, workholding, offsets, operator actions or production environment.

7.2 Before every production use, the Customer is responsible for determining that the NC program is correct, safe and suitable. Proving must be undertaken by competent personnel in accordance with the machine manufacturer’s instructions and the Customer’s risk assessments and safe systems of work.

7.3 Appropriate verification includes, as applicable:

review of the NC code and program limits;

CAM and machine simulation or graphics checks;

verification of machine kinematics, work offsets, tool data, limits, clearances, fixtures and workholding;

dry run, reduced rapid/feed, optional stop and single-block proving where appropriate; and

supervised first-off manufacture and inspection.

7.4 The Post Processor, CAM simulation and machine graphics must not be treated as the sole means of collision avoidance or machine safety. The Customer must not use unproved NC output in unattended or unsupervised production.

8. 90-Day Support and Modification Period

8.1 For 90 calendar days from the Delivery Date, the Supplier will provide reasonable remote support for the original Agreed Specification. This includes correction of reproducible defects, reasonable output refinements needed to achieve the agreed functionality and reasonable commissioning assistance where a post change is required.

8.2 Reasonable refinements may include NC formatting, minor logic, cycle formatting, tool-change behaviour and machine-specific output adjustments. The Supplier will reasonably determine whether a request is a defect/refinement or additional scope.

8.3 The support period excludes issues caused by machine or controller changes, software updates, parameter changes, hardware modifications, new tooling or accessories requiring new logic, incorrect CAM programming, machine setup or operator error, third-party faults, or modification of the Post Processor by anyone other than the Supplier.

8.4 Support is remote unless the quotation states otherwise. The Customer must provide timely access, files and information. The Supplier is not required to support obsolete or unsupported third-party software.
