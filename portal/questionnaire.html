CPS Portal Help

Customer – Stage 1

  • Sign in with your email code and choose or create the correct CPS project.
  • Your answers save automatically. You can sign out and return later.
  • Complete the machine, control, CAM, kinematics, safety and required-feature sections.
  • If simulation is required, a machine CAD model is mandatory.
  • Upload manuals, proven NC, a machine-at-home photo and other requested evidence.
  • Submit Stage 1 when complete. CPS will review the machine information, resolve any questions and agree the project scope before development starts.

CPS staff

Review submitted Stage 1 information in the internal dashboard. Resolve any questions, lock the agreed specification, agree the commercial scope and develop the post/simulation. Commissioning opens only when CPS publishes a test release. Do not progress while safety-critical machine information is assumed or contradictory.
