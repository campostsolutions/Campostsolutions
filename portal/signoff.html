Stage 3 – Customer acceptance

  • Review the completed commissioning result first.
  • Identify the exact post and/or simulation revision being accepted.
  • Record limitations, exclusions and anything not tested.
  • Select Accepted, Accepted with limitations, or Not accepted.
  • Not accepted returns the project to Stage 2 for further work/retest.

Accepted records are locked as the release baseline and can be printed/saved as PDF.
