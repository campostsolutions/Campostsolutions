Stage 2 – Commissioning

  • The checklist is generated from the saved Stage 1 machine configuration.
  • Record each item as Pass, Fail, Not tested or N/A, with notes where useful.
  • Tested NC/results and the CAM test project are mandatory evidence.
  • Choose Ready for sign-off only when the agreed scope is proven and no blocking issue remains.
  • Retest required keeps the project in Stage 2.
